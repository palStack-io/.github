# Contributing to palStack

Thanks for wanting to help.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Code contributions are paused** while we work out how to review AI-assisted changes well. We will reopen them.
> Until then, issues, bug reports, ideas and questions are very welcome.

## Report a bug

Open an issue on the pal's own repository:

- [finpal-core](https://github.com/palStack-io/finpal-core/issues)
- [pantrypal-core](https://github.com/palStack-io/pantrypal-core/issues)
- [propertypal-core](https://github.com/palStack-io/propertypal-core/issues)
- [clubpal-core](https://github.com/palStack-io/clubpal-core/issues) (gatherPal)
- [jobsearchPal](https://github.com/palStack-io/jobsearchPal/issues)
- [pointsPal](https://github.com/palStack-io/pointsPal/issues) (card data corrections; please link the issuer's page)

The most useful report says what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and which version or image tag
you run. Please keep personal data (real names, emails, bank details) out of issues and screenshots.

## Security problems

Never in a public issue. See the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Ideas and questions

Join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/mcgcp7RdYs), or email support@palstack.io.

## Be kind

Be respectful and assume good intent. We may remove comments or block accounts that harass people.
